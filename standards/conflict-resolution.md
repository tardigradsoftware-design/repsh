# Conflict Resolution

When sources/skills disagree — DO NOT HIDE IT.

## Decision order
1. **priority** (core > peripheral) 2. **authority** (official > maintainer > paper > tested-implementation > community > opinion) 3. **scope** (more specific governs its domain) 4. **recency** (newer verified beats older, same authority)

## Record format (mandatory)
```markdown
CONFLICT: <topic>
Source A (<authority>, <date>): says X
Source B (<authority>, <date>): says Y
Difference: <what actually differs — definitions? versions? context?>
Likely explanation: <why both exist>
Current recommendation: <what to do TODAY, with confidence label>
```
Both sources stay linked. `confidence: CONFLICTING` on the record until resolved by evidence.
